Note Tracker

Dated tracking records and quick note-taking with tags. Everything is stored locally in SQLite.

How it works: One device runs as the hub. Every other device sets its hub address here, then at day's end taps Push (send my new notes to the hub) and Pull (bring back the consolidated set). Notes are matched by content, so identical notes are never duplicated — different notes are all kept.
